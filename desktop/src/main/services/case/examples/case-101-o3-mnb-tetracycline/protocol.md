# Case-101 O3-MNB tetracycline degradation

## Experiment purpose
Verify the complete scientific workflow from raw experimental data import,
kinetic analysis, ELN recording and manuscript generation.

## Conditions
- Pollutant: Tetracycline
- Initial concentration: 20 mg/L
- Reactor volume: 5 L
- Ozone micro-nano bubble system
- Ozone flow: 1.5 L/min
- Temperature: 25 °C
- Initial pH: 7

## Measurements
- HPLC tetracycline concentration
- Dissolved ozone concentration
- TOC
- UV254
- DO
- ORP
- pH

## Expected results
- TC removal approximately 97%
- First-order kinetic fitting R² > 0.95
- Ozone dissolution enhanced by micro-nano bubbles
